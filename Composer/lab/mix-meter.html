Composer · mix meter

Listen (A/B)

The row composed with the defaults (seed 1), played through the player with the mix switches live. The level faders stay at their defaults (0.8), so MIDI would be unchanged by any of this.

Probe

Each stem alone (and the whole mix), rendered offline through the player. Peak and RMS in dBFS, true peak in dBTP, M-max, S-max and integrated in LUFS, GR (the master compressor's gain reduction while the mix plays) in dB.
